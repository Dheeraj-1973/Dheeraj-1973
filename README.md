## Hi, I'm Dheeraj Nallamotu

Final-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering (Honors, AI specialization) at KL University, India.
I'm interested in **Natural Language Processing** and **Human-in-the-Loop AI**: building systems that know when they are uncertain and when a human should step in.

### Research and projects

- **[Human-in-the-Loop Escalation Architecture for NLU Deployments](https://github.com/Dheeraj-1973/hitl-escalation-architecture)** (B.Tech capstone, team lead)
  Decides whether a conversational request can **PROCEED** automatically or must **ESCALATE** to a human reviewer, using confidence checks, operation-risk checks and critical-entity validation. Covers travel/airline, food ordering, e-commerce and appointment scheduling. Research paper in progress.
  `Python` `FastAPI` `Pydantic` `SQLAlchemy` `PostgreSQL/SQLite`

- **[FreshGuard](https://github.com/Dheeraj-1973/FreshGuard)**: human-in-the-loop decision support for perishable-food shelf-life monitoring (team lead and developer)
  First-author paper **accepted at ICACTCE '26**. Rule-based environmental-stress assessment over simulated temperature and humidity readings, alert deduplication, and a human review step (Accept / Reject / Escalate).
  `Python` `FastAPI` `SQLAlchemy` `SQLite` `HTML/CSS/JS`

- **Collaborative Agents Using CrewAI**: a multi-agent workflow where a coordinating agent delegates content generation, question answering and summarization to specialized agents and merges their outputs.
  `Python` `CrewAI` `LangChain`

- **[AI Resume Analyzer](https://github.com/Dheeraj-1973/AI-Resume-Architect-Enterprise)**: GenAI resume analysis with Gemini and Plotly visualizations.
  `Python` `Gemini API` `Plotly`
